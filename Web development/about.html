Our History

Located in the heart of Skoolstraat, Rietpoort, Smithfield Primary School has long stood as a foundational pillar for basic education in the Mohokare local municipality. As a Quintile 2 public primary school, we have dedicated ourselves to serving our local community by providing accessible, high-quality educational resources to generations of learners in the Free State.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide a safe, inclusive, and stimulating learning environment that empowers learners in Smithfield to reach their full academic and personal potential.

Our Vision

To be a leading primary education center in the Free State that nurtures resilient, innovative, and community-minded future leaders.
