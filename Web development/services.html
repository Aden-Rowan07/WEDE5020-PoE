Our Academic Phases

Foundation Phase (Grades R - 3)

Our foundation phase focuses on essential literacy, numeracy, and life skills. We use interactive and play-based learning methods to ensure children develop a love for learning in a nurturing environment.

Intermediate Phase and Senior Phase (Grades 4 - 7)

As learners progress to the intermediate phase and senior phase, we introduce a broader range of subjects, including Natural Sciences and Technology, to prepare them for senior phase education and independent thinking.

School Uniforms

Uniform Guidelines

Learners are required to wear the official school uniform to foster a sense of pride and equality.

  • Grey trousers or green skirts
  • White or Grey collared shirts
  • Green school jersey with the school badge
  • Black school shoes and white socks(For girls) or grey socks(For boys)
